    🌿 Cultural Orchard Hygiene

    Prune tree canopies for maximum 360° sunlight and air movement. Rake and compost or burn fallen infected leaves in autumn to eliminate overwintering scab pseudothecia.

    🧊 Bitter Pit & Yield Booster

    During Fruit Growth, apply Calcium Chloride @ 3–4g / Litre water (600–800g / 200L drum) foliar spray at 15-day intervals (4–5 rounds) to prevent corky Bitter Pit and enhance storage life.

    📒 Anti-Resistance Rotation

    Never apply DMI or strobilurin fungicides more than twice per season. Alternate chemical classes (Mancozeb, Dodine, Difenoconazole, Captan) to prevent fungicide resistance in Kashmir orchards.
