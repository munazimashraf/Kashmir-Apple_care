🌿 Canopy Airflow & Hygiene

Prune lower branches and water sprouts to ensure light penetration. Spray 5% Urea on leaf litter post-harvest to rapidly decompose scab-bearing leaves.

🧊 Bitter Pit Prevention Booster

During Fruit Growth, apply foliar Calcium Chloride at 3–4g per Litre (or Calcium Nitrate) at 15-day intervals to enhance cell wall firmness and cold storage life.

🐝 Honeybee Protection Protocol

Strictly avoid all chemical insecticide and acaricide applications during Full Bloom. Spray only after dusk or dawn when pollinators are inactive.
